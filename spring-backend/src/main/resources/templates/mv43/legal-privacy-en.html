WOLDEOK MONEYVERSE · PRIVACY POLICY

Woldeok Moneyverse processes personal data on a strict data-minimization basis, limited to authentication, ledger integrity, and security operations. This document details what data is collected, why it is processed, and our retention schedules.

Core Privacy Tenet

OAuth access/refresh tokens and raw email addresses are not stored long-term. Access IP addresses are encrypted and stored solely for fraud prevention and cyber incident response. Public pages may feature Google AdSense displays subject to Google's standard cookie and advertising policies.

1. Data Categories, Processing Purposes & Retention

We process only the minimum information necessary to provide services.

Category Collected Items Processing Purpose Retention Period
OAuth Login Provider ID, Display Name User authentication, anti-fraud Purged within 30 days of deletion
Virtual Ledger Internal UID, WLD transactions Simulation ledger reconciliation De-identified or retained up to 1 year
Security Audit Masked IP, User-Agent, API method DDoS mitigation, anomaly detection General logs 90 days; audit 1 year

2. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosures

Woldeok Moneyverse does not sell or lease user data to third parties. External integrations are limited to OAuth identity providers (Discord, Google) and hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare, Supabase).

3. Cookies & Session Management

We use secure HTTP-only session cookies strictly necessary to maintain authentication state. Third-party analytics and advertising cookies are managed under opt-out preferences.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users possess the right to view, correct, or permanently expunge their personal records at any time. Self-service deletion is accessible via Account Settings or by contacting the Privacy Officer.
