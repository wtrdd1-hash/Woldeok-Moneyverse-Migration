## Security/trust findings
High:
1. private-data leakage through personalized shares;
2. phishing/lookalike achievement/reward shares leading to ATO;
3. referral/share fraud via multi-account farming;
4. harmful UGC, impersonation, doxxing and malicious links.

Minimum conditions remain public-safe allowlists, private-by-default personalization, no secret/session/recovery values in URLs, official-domain cues, report/removal controls, no meaningful economy reward for raw clicks/shares/signups, and separate security/fraud QA before economic referral or broad public UGC rollout.
