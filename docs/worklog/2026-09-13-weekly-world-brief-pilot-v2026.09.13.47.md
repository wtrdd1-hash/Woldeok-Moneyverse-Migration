## Largest growth gap
The Weekly World Brief concept existed, but the first edition was not yet specific enough to prove a consumer return loop. Without a pilot structure it could degrade into a patch list, feature grid, finance-promotion surface or advertising page.

## Decision
Define a first-edition pilot around:

`one world change worth knowing → one explanation → one contextual continuation → first meaningful action/comeback → D1 continuity → D7 next edition`

Use one lead story, one learning takeaway, one dominant contextual CTA and one non-punitive next-week promise.
