# GitHub Update — v2026.10.04.523

## Summary
Planning authority now separates Moneyverse monetary and fiscal institutions.

- Central Bank: monetary-policy approval.
- Mint Bureau: execution-only issuance/retirement.
- Central Treasury: taxes, budgets and spending of existing WLD.
- Economy Core/Settlement Ledger: double-entry settlement, supply accounting and reconciliation.
- AI cannot directly mint/retire or monetize a treasury deficit.
- Taxes/treasury spending/fully-funded loans/Treasury bonds have zero total-supply effect.
- Canonical mint and retirement certificates are required for supply changes.
