# v2026.10.01.495 — Single Treasury & Social Tax Recirculation Design

This planning-only draft proposes a single central WLD treasury account with logical budget envelopes.

Key design changes:
- 100% of collected tax enters the central treasury; tax is never burned.
- Currency sinks, when needed, are separate disclosed non-tax policies.
- Treasury revenue is recirculated through welfare, public jobs, community projects, citizen participation dividends, market stabilization/item buybacks, and event budgets.
- Public spending transfers existing treasury WLD rather than minting new WLD.
- Reserve, reconciliation, anti-abuse, idempotency and double-entry constraints remain mandatory.
- Current multi-vault/tax-burn directions are documented as conflicts for later authority integration.

No runtime, database, Test, or Production change is included in this draft.
