## Important product decisions
- Wealth alone is not the progression model; resilience, free cash flow, skills, credit health and career/business health are co-equal.
- Initial lending is fully funded by existing WLD pools.
- AI personal finance is advice-first and cannot silently execute economic actions.
- Crises must have recovery paths and cannot create permanent new-user softlocks.
- Seasonal resets do not wipe core assets/debt/history.
- Public economic profiles are opt-in and hide exact balances/debts.
- Public calculators are utility-first SEO surfaces, not thin pSEO factories.
