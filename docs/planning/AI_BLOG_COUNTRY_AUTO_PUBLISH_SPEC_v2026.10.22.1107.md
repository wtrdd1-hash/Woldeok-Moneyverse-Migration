## Product decisions — what “publish immediately, delete later” means

1. **Country-original, not machine-translation mass posting**: KR (`ko-KR`), US (`en-US`), JP (`ja-JP`), CN mainland (`zh-CN`) each gets an independently researched and naturally written native article with its own query intent, applicable laws, source evidence, examples and calculator link. A common world topic can inform multiple distinct briefs; country, residency and language are separate settings. Do not force four variants when a topic applies to one country only.
2. **Automatic publish-first on safe eligibility**: On finishing AI draft, run deterministic *pre-publication* gates. An eligible low-risk, sourced evergreen explanation proceeds immediately and automatically through the new DB publication/outbox into the public read model, without waiting for a human approval queue or fixed scheduled time. “Immediately” means once automated checks, atomic commit and public read acknowledgement complete, **not posting raw unverified AI text**. Worker restart/retry must yield exactly one public version.
3. **Human review after publishing**: every auto-published edition enters mandatory `POST_PUBLICATION_REVIEW_DUE` queue. Reviewer may retain, correct by a new revision, unpublish/withdraw, or flag. Review target within 24 hours of publication; **if not reviewed within 24 hours, keep the article PUBLIC and send repeated scoped reviewer alerts; do not automatically unpublish, delete, noindex or withdraw for missed review SLA.** The time limit is a staff follow-up target, not a publication timeout. Only an authorized, explicit operator action (or separately justified emergency/legal security order) may withdraw a public article.
4. **Delete later, reversible with evidence**: An authorized moderator can remove any article immediately, without waiting for a second reviewer, and can batch-select suspicious articles (with safeguards). Remove it from public hub, internal recommendations, RSS/sitemap/BlogPosting and indexable projection, invalidate caches/CDN and mark withdrawal receipt. Choose HTTP `410 Gone` for genuinely removed articles or a dated correction page with `noindex` when truthful user notice is needed; do not silently redirect to an unrelated URL. Keep immutable version/audit history access-controlled, purge separately only where privacy/retention rules demand it. Search-engine deindexing is eventual, not guaranteed immediately by deletion.
5. **Source/country and safety outrank speed**: This post-first workflow applies *only* to GREEN. AMBER is prepublication human review; RED is blocked. Later deletion cannot undo exposure of false tax/pension/market facts.

## Risk-tier classifier (server-authoritative, default deny)

| Tier | Eligible content and pre-publish gate | Public action |
|---|---|---|
| GREEN — instant | Stable non-prescriptive financial literacy or basic arithmetic/compound-interest definitions, original country-specific examples, no unsupported current data or personal advice; official support for material claims; formula fixtures; native proofreading/language checks; 181 legacy/cross-market originality checks; policy/rights/SEO validation | **Auto publish after deterministic QA**; reviewer due in 24h, **alert only if overdue; keep public** |
| AMBER — review before public | Country-specific tax, retirement/pension eligibility, ISA/NISA/IRA/iDeCo limits, regulated product comparisons, dated interest/rates, public policy change, credit/debt sensitive recommendations, uncertain translation or jurisdiction, source conflicts | `REVIEW_REQUIRED` until country-qualified human editor approves exact revision; then authorized publisher may make public, with postpublication monitoring |
| RED — block | Unverifiable “latest” rate, invented performance or guaranteed returns, fabricated legal/numeric claim, missing source/rights, abusive scaled near-duplicate content, unsafe prompt injection/HTML/links, PII exposure, policy-disallowed or deceptive sponsored pages | `REJECTED` or `QUARANTINED`; never auto-publish or direct-index |

Automated QA pass cannot be granted by the same generative model's confidence score. Use independent deterministic rules and fixtures with fail-closed data; an AI examiner may suggest flags but cannot mint a receipt. Tier upgrades require source/evidence plus policy-approved reviewer; risk unknown defaults AMBER/RED as appropriate. A kill switch immediately disables auto-publication without making old API usable.

## Operational SLAs, acceptance and measured growth

Initial publication quota is **two genuinely useful, safely reviewed or GREEN-gated new original articles weekly across the whole site**, not four translations counted as four. Four country queues must be visible and given fair editorial attention; where evidence and reviewer capacity permit, aim for at least one qualifying original per market over a rolling four-week horizon. Never fabricate publish success to meet a quota. When a country has no GREEN posts, allow independent AMBER human review, without holding ready GREEN content in other markets.

Notifications: new instant post, reviewer 24h due and overdue, unpublish/withdraw, source expiry, legacy writer recurrence, duplicate, unsafe regulator claim and crawl indexing drift; avoid alerts for each ordinary successful substep. Operator can globally/per-country disable instant publish. Post-review can revise and relaunch under new immutable receipt only after appropriate checks.
