## 1. Gap selected this run

Moneyverse has increasingly strong retention mechanics, but the largest current acquisition/activation gap is upstream: **a first-time visitor can still see several plausible product identities at once — wallet, minigames, virtual stocks, shop, quests, community and sponsored inventory — before understanding one memorable reason to care.**

The public home currently says `community virtual economy service`, while the visible shortcut hierarchy strongly foregrounds wallet, games, exchange, shop and quests. That is truthful but broad. A broad category description is not yet a sharp consumer promise.

Selected loop:

`qualified discovery → one clear promise → one proof/sample → one authored interest → contextual signup → meaningful first action → D1 promise kept → D7 coherent identity → D30 durable history/share`

This is a positioning/content/funnel specification, not a navigation-component or backend implementation specification.

## 2. Consumer-facing category promise

Primary positioning:

**“A persistent community world where what you do becomes progress, collections, stories and shared history — using a game-only virtual economy.”**

The economy is a mechanic, not the entire identity. The first impression should lead with agency, persistence and community-world progression; WLD/WDX remain supporting systems with explicit game-only disclosure.

The product should not present itself as:
- an investment service;
- a savings or yield product;
- a gambling destination;
- a get-rich simulation;
- a generic Discord reward bot;
- an ad-supported portal of unrelated minigames;
- a real brokerage, bank or financial-learning substitute.

## 3. First 30 seconds: promise before inventory

A qualified first visitor should understand four things quickly:
1. **What this is:** a persistent community economy game/world.
2. **What I can become/do:** build a profession, collect/curate, follow a fictional world/company, contribute to a project or explore a season.
3. **What persists:** choices and achievements become personal history.
4. **What it is not:** WLD/WDX/rewards are virtual/game-only and not cash, deposits or securities.

Do not use the first viewport to ask the visitor to understand the full economy. Shortcut breadth may exist, but it should not substitute for a primary promise and one obvious next action.

## 4. First 3 minutes: proof of promise

Before signup, provide one bounded sample where practical. Candidate public-safe samples:
- a fictional company/world card with one explainable event;
- a collection/exhibit preview;
- a profession path preview;
- a season/project archive;
- a short educational simulation or replay excerpt;
- a public-safe community/project outcome.

The sample should end with one authored choice such as `follow this world`, `save this path`, `start this collection`, or `continue this project`.

Signup should preserve that intent. `Create account` is weaker than `Save this path and continue` when the latter is truthful.

## 5. Acquisition channel promise discipline

Every acquisition channel should make a promise that the first session can actually keep.

### Search / SEO
Search pages should solve the query first, then connect to one playable or followable thread. Do not use broad financial keywords to attract people expecting real investing, savings, yield or brokerage products.

### Social sharing
Share completed artifacts and stories rather than raw wealth. Good share objects include a collection chapter, profession milestone, project outcome, season archive, educational replay or public-safe world/company insight.

### Creator/community collaboration
Creator copy must accurately describe Moneyverse as game-only/simulated where economy or WDX content appears. Material sponsorship or other value exchange must be disclosed clearly.

### Paid acquisition
Judge channels on `qualified visit → meaningful activation → D7/D30 retained user → contribution`, not CPI/CPC or raw signup alone. Creative that produces high clicks but weak D7/D30 is a failed promise.

## 6. Brand pillars

Use four durable pillars to keep content coherent.

### Agency
The user chooses what to build, collect, learn or follow. Avoid mandatory feature tours as the brand experience.

### Persistence
Progress should leave a visible record. This connects first-session choice to D1/D7/D30.

### Shared world
Community and live content create context, but social pressure is not required for basic progression.

### Safe fiction
Virtual stocks, bank, loans and casino-like mechanics remain clearly fictional/game-only, bounded by existing integrity, probability, privacy and age/legal gates.

Any new campaign that cannot fit these pillars should explain why before adoption.

## 7. D1/D3/D7/D14/D30 promise ladder

### D1 — prove memory
Return to the exact thread or choice the user cared about. The first promise is broken if signup or a day boundary sends the user to an unrelated finance or feature grid.

### D3 — prove change
Show one real change, application or useful unchanged state inside that thread.

### D7 — prove coherent identity
The user should be able to answer `what am I doing here?` with something more specific than `earning WLD`. Examples: developing a profession, curating a collection, following a fictional issuer/world, building a project, learning a strategy.

### D14 — broaden voluntarily
Offer one adjacent system only when it reinforces the user's existing identity. Deferring should carry no penalty.

### D30 — prove durable history
The user's profile/history should contain at least one meaningful chapter, artifact or progression thread worth revisiting or optionally sharing.

## 8. Content and SEO architecture

Prioritize content clusters that reinforce the same product identity:
- beginner guides to the simulated economy and game systems;
- fictional company/world profiles with original lore and event explanations;
- profession/collection guides;
- season/event archives and retrospectives;
- educational glossary and simulation explainers;
- public-safe project/community outcomes.

Do not scale doorway pages around `ticker × date × keyword`, `user × balance`, `loan/casino outcome`, private portfolio, recovery/security state, referral code or thin auto-generated status pages.

Google's current people-first guidance asks whether a site has a clear primary purpose and whether users leave with a satisfying result. Naver's current title guidance similarly recommends that the main page title clearly express the site's nature/brand rather than chase search exposure through unstable or overlong titles. The brand/SEO contract should therefore be one coherent purpose, not many keyword identities.

## 9. Viral loop

Preferred viral loop:

`meaningful result → public-safe artifact/story → recipient understands context without login → optional sample → authored interest → signup only if useful → D7 retained thread`

Avoid:
- raw invite spam;
- wealth-flex cards by default;
- exact portfolio/debt/casino result sharing;
- reward-heavy referral copy;
- share-to-continue gates;
- session tokens, PII or private asset state in URLs/metadata.

Referral rewards, if any, remain delayed, capped, multi-day and primarily cosmetic/honor/convenience oriented. Raw registration or share events are not meaningful economy actions.

## 10. Monetization and sponsor fit

Monetization must reinforce rather than redefine the brand.

Acceptable after repeated value:
- clearly labeled public-content/native sponsorship;
- ad-removal subscription;
- non-P2W profile/space/exhibit/season cosmetics;
- archive/gallery expression;
- B2B2C or creator collaboration where relationship disclosures are clear.

Do not place a sponsored module so prominently that the visitor cannot distinguish the product's own promise. Ads must not look like buy/sell/loan/repay actions or factual market recommendations.

Creator/endorsement relationships must be conspicuous and understandable. FTC endorsement guidance remains a relevant U.S. reference: material connections should be disclosed, and endorsements must be truthful and non-misleading.

## 11. Funnel and KPI additions

Maintain existing metrics and add:
- first-viewport product-purpose comprehension;
- `what is Moneyverse?` unaided comprehension sample;
- promise → sample engagement;
- sample → authored-interest rate;
- authored-interest → contextual signup rate;
- signup → exact-intent recovery;
- time-to-first-value by acquisition promise;
- D1 promise-kept rate;
- D7 coherent-identity rate;
- D30 durable-history rate;
- share recipient → understood-context → meaningful activation;
- organic landing → D7/D30 retained-user conversion;
- paid CAC per D30 retained user;
- retention-adjusted revenue/contribution by acquisition message.

Guardrails:
- finance-like misunderstanding rate;
- bounce/backtracking after first viewport;
- fake signup/referral fraud;
- spam/report rate;
- ATO/phishing signal rate;
- privacy complaint rate;
- ad-induced churn;
- youth-safety complaints.

## 12. Experiment backlog

### A — persistent-world promise vs feature inventory headline
Hypothesis: a single persistent-world/identity promise improves qualified activation and D7 versus a broad feature inventory.
Target: new anonymous visitors.
Control: current broad community-economy + shortcut-forward presentation.
Treatment: promise → proof → one action.
Primary: meaningful activation and D7 retention.
Guardrails: signup conversion, misunderstanding, bounce, complaint.
Observation: at least one mature D7 cohort; D30 before permanent standardization.

### B — one pre-signup sample vs direct login CTA
Hypothesis: a useful public sample improves qualified signup and post-auth activation without weakening security boundaries.
Primary: visit → meaningful activation.
Guardrails: public data exposure, bot traffic, time-to-first-value.

### C — identity artifact sharing vs wealth/status sharing
Hypothesis: collection/project/mastery artifacts create higher-quality referral activation and lower abuse than raw wealth/status cards.
Primary: share recipient → D7 retained user.
Guardrails: privacy reports, referral fraud, spam, harassment.

### D — intent-matched SEO cluster vs broad finance-keyword acquisition
Hypothesis: narrower game/simulation/world intent yields lower traffic but higher activation/D30 quality.
Primary: organic D30 retained users per 1,000 visits.
Guardrails: misleading-finance query share, bounce, Search quality signals.

### E — sponsor after promise/sample vs sponsor before product understanding
Hypothesis: delaying sponsor prominence until after product comprehension improves retained contribution despite fewer early impressions.
Primary: D30 retained contribution.
Guardrails: ad-induced churn, accidental clicks, revenue per eligible user.

## 13. Security, abuse and privacy review

### HIGH — financial-service impersonation/misunderstanding
Scenario: acquisition copy makes WDX/WLD look like real investing, deposits, yield or gambling, or attackers impersonate that framing.
Impact: consumer harm, phishing susceptibility, legal/trust risk.
Minimum conditions: prominent game-only language where financially suggestive mechanics appear; no cash redemption/return guarantees; no real-market recommendation framing.
Separate QA/legal review: required for finance-adjacent paid/creator campaigns.

### HIGH — phishing/ATO through branded acquisition links
Scenario: fake `Moneyverse investment`, `WLD reward`, `portfolio alert` or creator/referral pages request credentials.
Minimum conditions: canonical domain/brand consistency; growth pages/messages never request passwords, OAuth codes or recovery codes; no secret/session/recovery material in URLs.
Separate QA: required for new deep links, creator landing pages, email/push campaigns.

### HIGH — public artifact/privacy leakage
Scenario: share/SEO pages expose balances, holdings, debt, casino history, private club/social graph, moderation or security state.
Minimum conditions: public-safe allowlist, private-by-default personalized pages, explicit share scope, noindex/authentication for private state.
Separate privacy/security QA: required before new personalized public pages.

### HIGH — referral/creator/bot acquisition abuse
Scenario: multi-account or automated traffic fabricates signups, shares or creator conversions for rewards.
Minimum conditions: no meaningful WLD/WDX for raw visit/signup/share; delayed/capped milestone rewards; retain fraud/eligibility/anomaly controls.

### MEDIUM — acquisition analytics overcollection
Scenario: cross-site ad/creator/SEO identifiers are joined with private economy/social history beyond need.
Minimum conditions: purpose limitation, data minimization, consent/legal basis as applicable, retention limits, no unrestricted export of sensitive/private state.

## 14. Legal/policy notes

- WLD/WDX remain virtual/simulated/game-only; no real securities, deposits, cash redemption or guaranteed returns are implied.
- Creator/sponsor relationships should be clearly disclosed where material connections exist.
- Personalized advertising or analytics should not treat private economy, debt, casino, security or under-age signals as free targeting data.
- Youth-facing discovery or community expansion requires age/safety review before broadening contact, targeting or probability/finance-like surfaces.
- Korea/U.S. privacy, advertising, subscription and consumer-protection review remains a release-time gate for affected campaigns.

## 15. Research note — 2026-09-14

Directly adopted:
- Roblox, 2026-06-15, `Optimizing Discovery`: discovery was expanded from a 7-day proxy toward a 28-day long-term retention view; adopt the principle that acquisition quality is judged by retained value, not quick clicks.
- Discord, 2026-08-20, game-discovery/social-play release: discovery, profiles and social context are tied to actual games/actions; adopt contextual discovery and meaningful downstream behavior rather than generic traffic.
- Google Search Central, current people-first guidance: maintain a clear primary purpose, original/substantial value and satisfying user outcome.
- Google Discover core update, 2026-02-05: reduce sensational/clickbait content and favor more original, timely, in-depth material.
- Naver Search Advisor, current 2026 guidance: main-page title should clearly express site nature/brand; search quality aims to surface authoritative/useful documents and suppress poor user-feedback/spam patterns.
- FTC endorsement guidance: endorsements should be truthful/non-misleading and material connections should be disclosed.

Reference only:
- Discord/Roblox metrics are their own platform/internal evidence and are not Moneyverse forecast numbers.
- Search and advertising guidance informs consumer planning but does not replace Korean/U.S. legal review.

## 16. Runtime Product Reality Audit — 2026-09-14

Verification: available for the public home.

Observed on `https://easy-scraping.com/`:
- the game-only WLD disclosure is visible and repeated;
- the title describes `Discord community virtual economy and game rewards`;
- the first visible shortcuts are wallet, five minigames, exchange, shop, quests and lobby;
- multiple `SPONSORED ADVERTISEMENT` placements are present;
- the core hero later says `a small, solid economy we build together` and calls Moneyverse a Discord-connected community virtual-economy service;
- first-time visitors are told they can preview activities before signup, but the page still exposes several competing product identities before one authored interest is established.

Conclusion: the public product is truthful about virtual value, but **category clarity and a single memorable first-visit promise remain unverified acquisition hypotheses.**

## 17. Decision for this version

Adopt a brand/discovery contract before expanding acquisition volume:

`one primary promise → one proof/sample → one authored interest → contextual signup → promise kept on D1 → coherent identity by D7 → durable history by D30`.

Do not expand broad finance-keyword acquisition, raw-signup referral rewards, wealth-first sharing, sponsor-first landing experiences or ambiguous investment-like creator messaging until this loop proves qualified D7/D30 growth without worsening trust, fraud, privacy or legal guardrails.
