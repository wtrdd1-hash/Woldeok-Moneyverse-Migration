# v2026.09.23.399 — Time-backed Jobs economy planning delta

## Decision
Move away from instant-completion WLD faucets. Participation remains unlimited, but system-funded WLD issuance is tied to authoritative elapsed time and one ordinary paid job slot per account.

Use a combined control model: hourly issuance × repeat decay × recurring sinks × money-supply feedback, rather than relying on a single daily cap. This preserves continued play while preventing click-frequency from becoming the dominant currency faucet.

Test-only seed: 300–600 WLD per verified real hour, advanced work up to 1.5×, low-complexity repeat payout 25–40% of baseline. Production constants require 7/30/90-day simulation using real wallet and sink data.

## Reference rationale
EVE Online continuously reports money supply, faucets/sinks and price indices. Unity's current economy guidance recommends combined metrics such as inflation, currency velocity, sink-to-faucet ratio and days-of-currency-on-hand. Large-scale OSRS research shows market interventions should be measured causally rather than tuned by intuition alone. GameDeveloper economy-design literature treats player time as a primary economic cost/control.

## Implementation gate
No Production promotion without server-authoritative minimum duration, one paid slot, instant-complete rejection, idempotent/concurrent settlement proof, restart-safe timers, repeat-history continuity, ledger reconciliation, economy telemetry and isolated exact-SHA Test.
