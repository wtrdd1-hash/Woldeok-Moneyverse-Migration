## 2. 6-Domain Planning Defect Analysis & Mitigation Contracts

### 🏛️ [Domain 1] Stock Exchange & Live Orderbook
- **G406-01 (P0) - Sequence Gap Recovery**: Implements monotonic `sequence_id` on all tick broadcasts. Clients maintain buffered deltas while fetching static snapshot; upon detecting a sequence gap (`incoming_seq > last_seq + 1`), the client invalidates local state and triggers automatic snapshot resynchronization.
- **G406-02 (P1) - KRX Standard 7-Tier Tick Size**: Enforces discrete price increments ranging from 1 WLD (< 1,000 WLD) to 1,000 WLD (>= 500,000 WLD) across 53 WLD (CHIPS) to 8.99M WLD (SPACE).
- **G406-03 (P1) - Multi-Factor Market Sentiment**: Combines AI news analysis with 24-hour price momentum, trading volume surges, and orderbook pressure ratios.

### 💰 [Domain 2] Virtual Economy Governance & Monetary Stabilization
- **G406-04 (P0) - OSRS GE Tax & Automated Item Sink**: Applies a 2% market transaction tax (capped at 500k WLD). 50% is permanently burnt (coin sink) and 50% is routed to the Treasury to algorithmically purchase and destroy floor-price items (item sink).

### 💬 [Domain 3] 1-on-1 Direct Messaging (P0)
- **G406-05 (P0) - Client UUID Idempotency & Cursor Pagination**: Eliminates duplicate messages during mobile reconnects via `(conversation_id, client_message_id)` unique constraints. Enforces strict cursor-based queries (`WHERE id < ? ORDER BY id DESC LIMIT 50`) to prevent message skipping or duplication.

### 🏦 [Domain 4] Banking, Fractional Reserves & Loan Risk
- **G406-06 (P0) - Aave Kinked Jump Rate Model & Basel III LCR**: Implements an $80\%$ optimal utilization threshold ($U_{\text{optimal}}$). Beyond $80\%$, borrowing rates sharply spike up to $48\%$ to deter capital drain and incentivize deposits. Retains a minimum $20\%$ statutory reserve buffer.

### 🔒 [Domain 5] Authentication, Session Continuity & Key Rotation
- **G406-07 (P1) - Dual-Key Grace Period Overlap**: Accepts both `CURRENT_KEY` and `PREVIOUS_KEY` over a 7-day rolling window to guarantee zero session invalidation during deployments or key rotation.

### 📱 [Domain 6] Mobile Fintech UX & Responsive Layout Engine
- **G406-08 (P1) - 44px Minimum Touch Target & Anti-Clipping**: Mandates `min-h-[44px]` for all interactive controls and `pb-[calc(env(safe-area-inset-bottom)+5rem)]` safe area padding across 320px to 1280px+ viewports.
