# Planning Delta v2026.09.25.440 — Administrator Mobile/Responsive Correctness

**Status:** PLANNING  
**Date:** 2026-09-25  
**Start/mid origin/main:** `5394dd266e68c0a3ebfee616cdf7f0d906116b48`

## Scope
This delta formalizes defects observed on the mobile Economy Operations administrator route and applies the resulting contract to all administrator routes.

## P0 decisions
- No page-level horizontal overflow on supported mobile widths.
- Navigation labels and controls may reflow/collapse but may not be silently clipped off-screen.
- AI Council/metric/control cards collapse to a one-column mobile flow.
- Slider thumb, displayed value, form state, submitted payload and server-authoritative saved value must agree.
- A control/value mismatch disables apply/save and is treated as a control-integrity failure.
- Required responsive coverage: 320/360/375/390/412/430 portrait, representative landscape, 768/1024 tablet and desktop, plus 200% and applicable 400% zoom/reflow.
- Every materially changed responsive/admin route requires at least five complete repeated QA passes.
- Any clipping, overlap, unreachable control, hidden CTA, body overflow, accidental value reset or value/state mismatch blocks Test acceptance and Production promotion.

## Authority
Detailed behavior is defined by `ACCESSIBILITY_RESPONSIVE_INTERACTION_SPEC.md` and its Korean counterpart.

## Scope truth
Documentation/planning only. This delta does not claim that the reported runtime UI defects are already fixed.
