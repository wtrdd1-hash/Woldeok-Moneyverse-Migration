## Superseding decisions

- Product/public fallback locale is Korean, not English.
- GeoIP is used to recommend/preselect a published language on indexable public pages; it does not force crawlers or explicit locale URLs to another language. Non-indexable app onboarding may use it as a first-session default.
- Overseas rollout is quality-first: EN/JA, then DE/FR/ES/pt-BR one locale at a time.
- Real site search performance and market keyword-volume estimates are distinct evidence classes. Synthetic GSC fallback data is prohibited from operational decisions.
- Programmatic SEO is independent-value-gated and has no fixed page-count success target.
- International product scope adds public utilities, virtual-economy tools, localized onboarding, timezone/event support, UGC translation/discovery and retention surfaces.
- Search acquisition covers text, Images, video and supplemental Discover with canonical/hreflang/CWV/internal-link quality gates.
- Cash monetization remains advertising-only. International profitability uses realized Page RPM/revenue per qualified organic session and incremental operating cost.
