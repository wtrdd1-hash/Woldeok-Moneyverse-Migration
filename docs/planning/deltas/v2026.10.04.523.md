## Decisions
- Central Bank owns monetary-policy approval and issuance/retirement envelopes.
- Mint Bureau is execution-only and requires a valid policy order plus exactly-once certificate.
- Central Treasury owns tax, fiscal cash, budgets and spending of existing WLD.
- Economy Core/Settlement Ledger owns double-entry settlement, supply accounting and reconciliation.
- Treasury reserves are fiscal-liquidity reserves, not mint authority.
- Taxes, transfers, treasury spending, fully funded loans and Treasury bonds do not change total WLD.
- Initial banking remains existing-WLD funded; deposit-money creation is deferred.
- AI cannot approve/execute minting or monetize a treasury shortfall.

## P0 implementation gates
1. Classify every WLD balance-increase and burn path.
2. Replace ambiguous injection semantics with explicit mint/treasury/reversal/correction types.
3. One monetary order + one mint certificate per issuance; one retirement certificate per supply reduction.
4. Independent money-supply and treasury reconciliation with zero unexplained variance.
5. Unauthorized mint paths and AI-to-Mint execution fail closed.
6. Exact-SHA isolated Test backend/API/DB evidence before Production.
