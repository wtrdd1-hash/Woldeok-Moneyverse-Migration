## Superseding decision

- Treasury tax/fee inflow is not a hard sink and healthy eligible surplus must not accumulate without a planned use.
- A fail-closed Treasury Recycling Engine may convert only eligible surplus into pre-approved program commitments while protected reserve, stability buffer and outstanding obligations remain covered.
- The tax portfolio is expanded into separate sale/listing/reprice/profit/luxury/property/business/seasonal policy classes so each behavior can be measured and tuned separately.
- Ordinary P2P transfers remain 0% by default; baseline rewards, refunds and starter/core access remain protected from taxation.
- Recycled treasury WLD funds visible participation loops rather than arbitrary direct grants.

## Safety

- no idle-balance tax, confiscatory wealth tax or arbitrary holding caps;
- no reward proportional to tax amount paid;
- no tax-funded lottery/jackpot/wagering;
- no automatic program may mint WLD or breach protected reserve;
- wash/self-transfer/related-account loops receive no program credit;
- reserve/reconciliation/data/integrity failure disables automatic discretionary commitment.
