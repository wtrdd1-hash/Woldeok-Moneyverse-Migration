## Core design decisions
- Retention = voluntary return + meaningful progress + social/world continuity + safe recovery.
- Multi-horizon goal stack: now/today, weekly, season, 90–365+ day legacy.
- Autonomy, competence and relatedness are explicit design requirements.
- Real-world absence alone cannot cause irreversible debt spiral, business collapse or total season loss.
- Dormant return uses recap, backlog compression, recovery, new opportunity and path choice.
- Notifications are contextual opt-in, category-controlled, quiet/digest capable and frequency-capped.
- Fake countdowns, false scarcity, permanent streak destruction, shame, forced ads and hidden recurring loss are prohibited.
- Event-trigger retention and lifecycle/cohort analysis are mandatory.
