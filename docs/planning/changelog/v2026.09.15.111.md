## New P0/P1 planning delta

### STOCK-111-01 — P0 release-truth / P1 user correctness — OPEN

**Problem.** A simulated market can appear healthy while its authoritative price scheduler is stopped. The 2026-09-15 incident proves configuration presence was an implicit runtime dependency and ordinary build/typecheck evidence was insufficient to prove live-market behavior.

**User impact.** Portfolio values, stock alerts, market discovery, comparative charts, educational explanations and any stock-linked retention loop can become misleading or inert. A frozen market also makes downstream QA produce false conclusions about trading behavior.

**Required design.** Treat market motion as an observable service contract, not merely process startup. Record scheduler-enabled state, last successful tick time, tick sequence/market epoch, latest authoritative price timestamp and per-symbol stale age. Public/user surfaces must distinguish `live`, `delayed/checking`, and intentionally `paused`; they must never claim a live market from cached prices after the configured freshness threshold. An operator kill switch remains explicit and auditable. Production default-on is acceptable only with liveness detection and fail-visible UX.

**Security/abuse.** Do not expose internal host/process topology or secrets in status payloads. Ticker controls are privileged operations with recent reauthentication/audit. Replayed/duplicate ticks must not duplicate economic value or bypass authoritative trade pricing. Detect abnormal tick frequency, clock reversal, impossible price jumps and stale-price order attempts. Market manipulation/collusion controls remain independent of ticker liveness.

**API/data backlog.** Existing stock read APIs should expose only safe market freshness metadata needed by clients; authoritative trade execution must reject or safely suspend orders when market freshness violates the trading contract. Persist/observe a monotonic tick identity or equivalent idempotent marker; do not mutate historical ledger records to repair ticker downtime. Any DB change requires a new migration and real-DB concurrency tests.

**UX.** Stock list/detail/portfolio/alerts show delayed/checking state with timestamp when stale. Do not fabricate movement. On comeback, summarize resumed market activity without implying real investment returns. WLD/WDX remain game-only.

**QA.** Unit: missing/true/false flag and freshness boundaries. Integration: scheduler start/stop/restart, duplicate tick, clock skew, DB failure, websocket/broadcast failure. E2E: visible stale state, order behavior during stale market, recovery without duplicate rewards/orders. Real-DB: concurrent ticks/trades and ledger reconciliation. Security: unauthorized pause/resume, BOLA on alerts/portfolio, rate/business-flow abuse. Release gate: exact-SHA Test must demonstrate at least two authoritative ticks separated by the configured interval and then demonstrate fail-visible stale behavior by stopping a synthetic Test ticker.

**Observability.** `market_ticker_enabled`, `market_last_tick_age_seconds`, `market_tick_success_total`, `market_tick_failure_total`, `market_stale_symbols`, `market_stale_trade_reject_total`, `market_broadcast_lag_seconds`; alert on freshness breach and repeated tick failure.

**Rollback.** Revert application/config to last-known-good immutable image/config while preserving incident evidence. Never backfill synthetic price movement into ledger history merely to hide downtime.

**Business.** Direct revenue: 0. Indirect value: protects stock engagement/retention, trust, alert usefulness and prevents support/fraud/dispute cost. Scale criterion is lower stale-market exposure without D1/D7/D30 or session-quality regression; kill any monetization experiment that benefits from misleading stale prices.

### SEO/public-market policy reinforcement

Index only durable, useful virtual-company/market educational pages with server-renderable unique explanatory content. Live prices may be displayed, but price-only or ticker×date pages are not indexable acquisition inventory. Canonical URLs remain stable by virtual-company identity; private portfolio, alerts, account, trades and personalized holdings are `noindex` and sitemap-excluded. Market freshness metadata must not leak user holdings or internal topology.
