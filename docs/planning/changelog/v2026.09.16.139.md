## Architecture decision

- Added a permanent dual-lane economy architecture: classical/deterministic Lane A and AI/learned Lane B run from the same immutable economy snapshot.
- Lane A remains the operational fallback and authority for accounting/reconciliation, hard constraints, deterministic market price formation, bounded policy envelopes and integrity containment.
- Lane B supplies LLM behavior agents, RL/MARL simulation, anomaly reasoning, counterfactuals, demand/product hypotheses and adversarial critique.
- Strong disagreement becomes `SHADOW`, `NO_OP` or human review rather than a blended live action. Agreement only makes a candidate eligible for Scenario Lab and deterministic validation.
- Added explicit domain authority for stock prices, shop repricing, SKU generation, profession/daily limits and faucet/sink tuning.
