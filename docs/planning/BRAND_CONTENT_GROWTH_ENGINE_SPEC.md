## 1. Gap selected

The largest remaining consumer-growth gap is a durable reason to revisit Moneyverse on days when a user does not intend to play a full session.

The existing stack already defines pre-signup value, D1–D30 return reasons and a retention-to-viral artifact loop. What remains weak is a repeatable public content cadence that can create first visits, branded/direct/organic returns, season anticipation, comeback context and long-term world attachment without relying on FOMO or task pressure.

Target loop:

`useful public content → curiosity about a living virtual world → contextual sample/exploration → signup or comeback → meaningful action → personal progress/artifact → future content revisit`

Content must remain useful even when the visitor never signs up.

## 2. Brand promise

**“A living virtual economy where you can learn, build, collect, and leave a history.”**

Editorial tone emphasizes curiosity, agency, learning, identity, world-building and reflection. Avoid getting-rich, real-market-beating, guaranteed-return, gambling-style or loss-threat positioning.

WLD/WDX, banking and market concepts remain clearly virtual/simulated/game-only and are not real securities, deposits, fiat money or promised investment returns.

## 3. Consumer content pillars

### 3.1 Weekly Moneyverse World Brief
A sustainable 1–3 minute recurring anchor containing roughly 3–5 meaningful changes: fictional-company/sector developments, season/world changes, collection/lore, club/city/community highlights, one learning takeaway and one optional next action.

Do not turn it into a daily obligation or imply loss for late readers. Preferred CTAs are contextual: explore the virtual company, try the simulated scenario, see the collection story, or catch up on the season.

### 3.2 Virtual Company & Market Stories
Explain what changed, why it matters inside the fictional world, what uncertainty exists, one reflection question and one relevant simulated experience. Do not publish buy/sell-now, guaranteed-winner or loss-recovery trading language. Profit-only rankings and trade-frequency challenges are not editorial goals.

### 3.3 Money Skills Lab
Create 3–5 minute learning content on diversification, concentration, risk/reward, loss review, journaling, budgeting, opportunity cost, supply/demand, fictional events, and virtual-economy fees/sinks/transfers. Optimize for understanding and meaningful next action, not transaction volume.

### 3.4 Season Anticipation & Archive
Use the existing D-14/D-7/D-3/D-1 preview ladder only when each step adds new context:
- D-14: theme/world premise;
- D-7: company/job/business/collection preview;
- D-3: community/city/club opportunities and catch-up;
- D-1: concise start guide;
- launch: new/returning/active entry paths;
- post-season: permanent archive, retrospective, Hall of Fame and collection history.

No “log in now or lose everything” framing. Mid-season and returning users need readable catch-up paths.

### 3.5 Collection & Lore Spotlight
Connect collections, restoration, engraving, spaces, professions/business identity, museums/archives, city sponsorship and landmarks to aspiration and self-expression. The product question is “why would I want this and what story can it express?” rather than “how can we remove more WLD?”

### 3.6 Community / Club / City Spotlight
Use opt-in public-safe stories about completed projects, creative public spaces, educational retrospectives and moderated creator/community challenges. Never expose private contact details, hidden membership data, private balances/portfolios, moderation state or inferred sensitive traits.

## 4. Cadence without content debt

Baseline cadence:
- one Weekly World Brief as the recurring anchor;
- one or two deeper stories/learning pieces in a normal week when quality justifies them;
- season preview content around real transitions;
- permanent archives for durable stories;
- no daily filler requirement.

Missing a publishing day must never damage player progression. Quality and reader value outrank URL volume.

## 5. Lifecycle role

- First visit: explain what Moneyverse is, why this page is useful now, what is simulated, and one related preview before signup pressure.
- D1: show one short piece tied to the path already chosen.
- D3: use story continuation, collection/lore or beginner learning to help form preference and identity.
- D7: make the Weekly World Brief the anchor for “what changed in the world + what can I continue.”
- D14: deepen season/company/club/city/collection context.
- D30+: connect personal archive with world archive so long-term users remember what they built during a period.
- Lapsed: summarize what changed, what remains theirs and one easy restart point; do not advertise missed losses.

## 6. Funnel and KPI model

Primary funnel:

`search/social/direct/share → useful content → engaged value → contextual sample → signup/comeback → meaningful action → D1 → D7 → D30`

Retention loop:

`active/returning user → recurring brief/story → meaningful action → personal progress → archive/share artifact → later content revisit`

Key metrics:
- content-assisted signup, activation and D1/D3/D7/D14/D30;
- time from content entry to first meaningful action;
- 7/30-day content revisit;
- Weekly World Brief reader → meaningful action;
- content-reader → comeback rate;
- branded organic/direct returning share;
- D7/D30/LTV by content pillar/source;
- share → engaged visit → pre-signup value → activation → D7;
- ARPU/ARPDAU, sponsor/ad revenue per engaged content user, subscription conversion, ad-induced churn, LTV/CAC and contribution margin;
- trust guardrails: fake-signup, referral/engagement fraud, ATO signals, spam/report, privacy complaints, suspicious duplicate rewards, sponsor-disclosure complaints and takedown/escalation rate.

## 7. SEO strategy

Priority clusters:
1. beginner simulated-economy/finance-learning questions;
2. virtual-company and fictional-market explainers;
3. season previews, launch guides and archives;
4. glossary/explainers with real educational value;
5. collection/lore/world-building;
6. substantial opt-in community showcases.

Rules:
- each indexable page must satisfy identifiable reader intent without requiring signup;
- EN and KO versions must each provide natural standalone value;
- do not mass-produce thin issuer/profile/recap pages to inflate index count;
- no keyword stuffing, cloaking, doorway pages or misleading functionality;
- editorial/sponsor/UGC origin must be understandable;
- update dates only when meaningful content changes;
- private account, portfolio, balance, billing, security, recovery, moderation and internal risk pages remain outside public search.

Organic success path is `qualified click → engaged read → contextual sample → signup/comeback → activation → D7/D30 → contribution margin`, not index volume.

## 8. Viral and social connection

Public editorial may be shared independently. Personal artifacts remain opt-in under `RETENTION_TO_VIRAL_GROWTH_LOOP_SPEC`.

Prioritize learning insights, world/company stories, collection/lore, season chapters, community achievements and owner-first progress. Do not make balance, profit, debt, casino outcome or private portfolio the default social card. Shared URLs/cards must not contain session secrets, recovery data, private asset details or hidden social identifiers.

## 9. Monetization after value

Good fits after readers receive value:
- clearly labeled contextual display/native sponsorship;
- ad-free subscription;
- non-P2W profile/space/archive/collection presentation products;
- sponsor-supported educational/lore series with editorial separation;
- clearly disclosed non-competitive B2B/B2B2C community/city sponsorship.

Sponsors cannot purchase favorable WDX outcomes/editorial conclusions, ranking/league advantage, banking/loan advantage, moderation priority, disguised organic endorsement or placements that imitate buy/sell/loan/repay controls.

Revenue decisions must include D7/D30, ad-induced churn, subscription cancellation, content/moderation/support cost and contribution margin.

## 10. Experiment backlog

### A. Contextual Weekly World Brief CTA vs generic Play/Sign up
Hypothesis: story-specific exploration creates more qualified action.
Target: anonymous/returning brief readers.
Primary: content → meaningful action/signup/comeback.
Guardrails: bounce, D7, privacy/spam complaints, fake signup.
Minimum observation: at least two weekly cycles plus D7 maturation and adequate cohort size.
Success: expand to strong pillars. Failure: simplify and retain only high-intent contexts.

### B. 30-second simulated sample before signup vs direct signup
Target: first-time organic beginner/learning visitors.
Primary: activation and time-to-first-value.
Guardrails: fake signup, errors, privacy complaints, D7.
Minimum observation: stable activation comparison plus D7 maturation.
Success: expand to high-intent clusters. Failure: keep content standalone and remove weak interaction.

### C. D-14/D-7/D-3/D-1 season context vs single announcement
Primary: season return/reactivation and first meaningful season action.
Guardrails: opt-out/report, pressure signal, D7/D30.
Minimum observation: full preview-to-launch window plus mid-season catch-up cohort.
Success: retain/add depth. Failure: reduce touchpoints but preserve archive/catch-up.

### D. Opt-in curated community spotlight vs generic project promotion
Primary: club/project exploration and meaningful participation.
Guardrails: harassment, doxxing/privacy complaints, report/takedown.
Minimum observation: multiple spotlights to observe moderation patterns.
Success: expand carefully. Failure: restrict to staff-authored public-safe summaries.

### E. Sponsor after first useful section vs top-of-page
Primary: revenue per engaged content session and sponsor yield.
Guardrails: bounce, completion, D7/D30, ad-induced churn, accidental clicks/trust complaints.
Minimum observation: enough sessions for both revenue and downstream retention comparison.
If early placement lifts short-term revenue but harms retention/trust, reject it.

## 11. Security, abuse and privacy review

### High — public/private boundary leakage
Impact: private balances, positions, account existence, social relationships or security context may enable stalking, phishing or targeted account attacks.
Minimum protection: explicit public scope/opt-in, public-safe fields only, no private balance/portfolio/security/recovery/moderation/risk data, clear hide/remove/redact path.
Separate development/QA: required before new personalized public surfaces.

### High — community spotlight / UGC impersonation, phishing or doxxing
Minimum protection: participant consent, moderation/report/takedown, no forced real identity, safe outbound-link policy, no private contact details.
Separate development/QA: required before scaling user-submitted spotlights.

### High — sponsor/creator deception
Minimum protection: clear local-language ad/sponsor/material-connection disclosure near content, editorial independence, no paid “signal” or guaranteed-return language.
Separate review: product/legal/trust gate before launch.

### Medium — bot/SEO/referral engagement fraud
No meaningful WLD/WDX reward for page views, shares or raw signup; use fraud-adjusted attribution. Separate fraud QA if paid acquisition or economic rewards attach to these signals.

### Medium — analytics/ad privacy overcollection
Use purpose-limited analytics; never send tokens, credentials or private financial-game fields to third-party analytics. Personalized ads require age/region/privacy review and conservative minor handling.

### Medium — phishing/ATO pressure through comeback content
No balance/loss threats, secret-bearing links or sensitive asset details in share/notification text. Actual deep-link/reengagement implementation requires security QA.

Existing auth/session/RBAC/ledger/admin/privacy boundaries remain unchanged.

## 12. Legal / policy cautions

- WLD/WDX remain virtual/simulated/game-only.
- Do not frame fictional-company stories as real investment recommendations or guaranteed returns.
- Sponsored/native content and compensated creator relationships require clear Korea/U.S.-appropriate disclosure.
- Community spotlights require appropriate publication/privacy consent.
- Personalized advertising, new tracking vendors, minor targeting, real-value referral rewards or major UGC expansion require separate privacy/legal/trust review.
- Any move toward real securities, deposits, cash redemption or real financial products is outside this spec and requires separate legal/product review.
