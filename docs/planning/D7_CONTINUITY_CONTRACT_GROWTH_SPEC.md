## 1. Gap selected

The largest remaining retention gap is not lack of content or lack of features. It is the missing contract between one meaningful visit and the next one.

The Weekly World Brief pilot already defines a useful first edition and a non-punitive next-week promise. The next question is whether the promise is specific enough to create D7 return behavior without becoming clickbait, a streak threat, a reward countdown, or a finance-like teaser.

Production reality rechecked on 2026-09-13:
- the public home is reachable and carries several sponsored placements;
- the home and `/announcements` still have no published operations/world-news content;
- the getting-started guide remains broad and wealth-forward, foregrounding deposits, bonds, loans, stock gains, passive income, business dividends and casino/minigames;
- recent v2026.09.13.48–49 work improves app/local/OAuth authentication readiness, but growth must not treat authentication success alone as activation;
- therefore the next planning priority is a narrow continuity contract: one topic remembered, one question carried forward, one easy return path, and one meaningful action after return.

Canonical loop:

`useful story or action → explicit continuity question → remembered thread → D1 continuity cue → D3 related deepening → D7 answer/new state → meaningful continuation → next continuity question`

## 2. The D7 continuity contract

Every weekly return-driving experience should make a small, truthful promise the product can actually keep.

A valid continuity contract contains four elements:

1. **Thread** — what the user is following: fictional company, profession, collection, season, city/community project, or learning theme.
2. **Open question** — what is genuinely unresolved or expected to change.
3. **Return window** — when a useful update is reasonably expected, without fake urgency.
4. **Resolution** — the next visit must visibly answer, update or close the prior question before introducing unrelated novelty.

Example:
- Thread: a fictional logistics company faces a supply constraint.
- Open question: does the company adapt by changing routes or absorbing higher costs?
- Return promise: next weekly brief will show what changed.
- D7 resolution: explain the new state, what trade-off occurred, and one optional next action.

Do not use:
- `Come back tomorrow or lose...`;
- unexplained countdowns;
- guaranteed rewards for opening content;
- fake cliffhangers where no meaningful state will change;
- `this stock will rise/fall next week`;
- loss-recovery, safe-yield or guaranteed-return framing.

## 3. First 30 seconds / 3 minutes / first session

### First 30 seconds
A newcomer should understand:
- Moneyverse is a fictional/game-only economy;
- one current world thread is worth following;
- the page can be useful before signup;
- there is one obvious next action, not a feature wall.

### First 3 minutes
The user should:
- finish one story/learning block;
- choose whether the thread is personally interesting;
- see one contextual continuation;
- know what question may be answered next time.

### First session
If signup/login happens, preserve topic continuity when safe. Do not drop the user into an unrelated generic feature grid merely because authentication succeeded.

Meaningful activation still requires a real user-value action such as following/saving a thread, completing one profession or learning action, beginning a collection/season path, recording a simulated reflection, or selecting a next goal.

## 4. Cohort-specific return reason

### D1 — recognition
Promise: **“The thing you chose is still here.”**

Show the remembered thread or last meaningful action before generic discovery. Do not lead with missed rewards.

### D3 — relevance
Promise: **“Moneyverse is beginning to reflect what interests you.”**

Offer one related explainer, collection, profession step, or world development. Avoid inferring real-world financial sophistication, creditworthiness, personality or sensitive traits.

### D7 — resolution
Promise: **“The question we left open now has an answer or a meaningful update.”**

The new edition should explicitly reference the prior thread and show:
- what changed;
- what did not change;
- why it matters in the fictional world;
- one useful concept or reflection;
- one next action;
- optionally, the next honest open question.

### D14 — expansion
Connect the same thread to a larger season, world, collection, profession, city or community arc.

### D30 — history
Show how repeated threads became part of a personal archive, identity, collection or long-term aspiration. The retention reason should be accumulated meaning, not fear of lost progress.

## 5. Session modes

### 1–3 minute quick check
- resolve one open question;
- see one change;
- choose one next action.

### 5–15 minute meaningful session
- act on the resolved thread;
- complete one milestone;
- save a reflection or curate a collection/history artifact.

### 30+ minute deep session
- profession/business planning;
- collection curation;
- archive/lore exploration;
- space/museum/headquarters identity work;
- club/city project participation.

Long sessions must not be required merely to avoid punishment.

## 6. Acquisition, SEO and brand implication

The continuity contract can strengthen acquisition only if each public edition is independently useful.

SEO funnel:

`qualified impression → useful answer/story → remembered thread → contextual preview/signup → activation → D7 resolution → D30 → retention-adjusted contribution`

Rules:
- publish only editions with original explanatory value;
- use accurate titles that describe the actual question/update;
- do not manufacture weekly freshness by changing dates without substantive change;
- do not mass-produce thin AI variants, doorway pages, per-user recap URLs or keyword pages;
- financial-learning topics need stronger trust, sourcing and game-only context;
- private balance, portfolio, debt, security, recovery, moderation and admin data remains private/non-indexable.

Google Discover/Search can be a supplemental discovery channel, but impressions are not the goal. The product metric remains qualified continuation and retained behavior.

## 7. Social and viral loop

The share object should be the resolved story, learning insight, collection/world change or public-safe project outcome.

A recipient should be able to understand the object without an account and then optionally follow the same thread.

Do not include by default:
- balance, holdings, debt or loan state;
- casino outcomes;
- private club/friend graph;
- account existence/security/recovery state;
- session/auth identifiers.

Referral rewards remain downstream-retention based and non-P2W. No meaningful economy reward for views, shares or raw registrations.

## 8. Monetization placement

Continuity is more valuable than an extra early impression.

Rules:
- resolve the promised question before monetization becomes visually dominant;
- do not place ads between the question and its answer;
- no ad should mimic the continuation CTA;
- sponsor modules must be labeled and cannot purchase editorial conclusions, WDX sentiment, rankings, loan terms or moderation outcomes;
- ad-removal subscription should be offered after repeated value is experienced, not as relief from intentionally degraded UX;
- recurring subscription terms must be clear, express/informed consent must precede charges, and cancellation must be straightforward.

Primary business metric: **retention-adjusted contribution**.

## 10. Experiment backlog

### Experiment A — resolved continuity vs unrelated freshness
Hypothesis: explicitly resolving last week's thread improves D7 meaningful return more than presenting entirely new stories.
Target: readers who consumed a prior weekly edition.
Control: fresh stories with no prior-thread reference.
Treatment: prior question resolved first, then new discovery.
Primary: D7 return → meaningful action.
Guardrails: dissatisfaction, bounce, perceived repetition.
Minimum observation: 4 publication cycles and D7 maturation.
Success: retain resolution-first hierarchy. Failure: shorten resolution block but keep explicit continuity cue.

### Experiment B — one specific question vs vague “come back next week”
Hypothesis: a concrete but non-sensational question improves D7 return quality.
Primary: D7 next-edition return and engaged read.
Guardrails: misleading-teaser complaints, FOMO/pressure feedback.
Minimum observation: 4 cycles.

### Experiment C — preserve origin after auth vs generic home
Hypothesis: returning the user to the originating safe thread after auth improves signup-to-activation and time-to-first-value.
Primary: signup/login → meaningful action.
Guardrails: auth abandonment, unsafe redirect/deep-link signals, post-login bounce.
Minimum observation: D7-matured auth-assisted cohorts.
Note: this is a consumer-experience experiment definition, not a new auth implementation contract.

### Experiment D — continuity notification vs reward urgency
Hypothesis: “the question you followed has an update” produces healthier comeback than “reward expires” language.
Primary: notification-assisted comeback → meaningful action.
Guardrails: opt-out, phishing reports, pressure complaints.
Minimum observation: only after notification/privacy/legal readiness; multiple send cycles.

### Experiment E — answer-first monetization vs ad interruption
Hypothesis: resolving the promised value before ads improves retention-adjusted contribution.
Primary: retention-adjusted contribution.
Guardrails: bounce, accidental clicks, CWV, D7/D30, ad complaints.

## 11. Security, abuse and privacy review

### High — continuity-link phishing / account takeover
User impact: credential theft and session compromise.
Abuse scenario: attackers imitate “your followed thread has an update” and route users to fake login pages.
Minimum protection: consistent official-domain cues; no credential/balance-loss urgency; no sensitive account data in notification copy; no secret/session/recovery material in share/deep links; auth destinations must preserve existing security boundaries.
Separate development/QA: required before email, push, external deep-link or personalized campaign rollout.

### High — public/private context leakage
User impact: targeted phishing, stalking, embarrassment or financial-game profiling.
Abuse scenario: continuity pages reveal a user's balance, holdings, debt, hidden social graph, security/recovery state or private activity.
Minimum protection: public-safe content only; personalized public sharing opt-in; hide/delete controls; no sensitive economy/security data in URLs or analytics payloads.
Separate development/QA: required before personalized public continuity surfaces.

### High — finance-like editorial drift
User impact: users mistake simulation content for investment, deposit, credit or guaranteed-return advice.
Abuse scenario: weekly questions become “which stock wins next,” “safe yield,” “loss recovery,” or “guaranteed passive income.”
Minimum protection: nearby game-only disclosure; editorial review; uncertainty language; no real-investment positioning or guaranteed-return claims.
Legal/product review: required for any proposal involving real value, cash redemption or real financial characteristics.

### Medium — algorithmic identity overreach
Do not infer real creditworthiness, wealth, investment risk tolerance, health, politics or other sensitive traits from followed threads. Personalization should remain explainable, editable and rejectable.

### Medium — engagement farming
No meaningful WLD/WDX for reading, returning, sharing or raw signup. Any future referral benefit must depend on fraud-adjusted retained milestones and remain non-P2W.

## 12. Legal/policy notes

- WLD/WDX remain virtual/simulated/game-only. No cash redemption, real deposit, security, gambling payout or guaranteed investment return is implied.
- U.S. subscription/negative-option policy remains active and evolving; launch-time legal review is required for recurring paid products.
- Youth/personalized-ad rules remain a moving area. Do not make minor-targeted personalized advertising a default growth strategy.
- Sponsored/native content must be clearly distinguishable from editorial content and sensitive economy actions.

## 15. Next growth priority

Do not add another content category next. Validate the continuity contract across several real editions:

`question remembered → D1 recognition → D7 honest resolution → meaningful action → next question`

Only after this loop improves D7/D30, trust and retention-adjusted contribution should Moneyverse scale publication frequency, notification reach, sponsorship or additional public-content inventory.
