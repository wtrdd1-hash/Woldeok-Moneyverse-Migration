## Why this changed

The recovered live service revealed a material consumer-story mismatch. The public home invites newcomers to inspect activities first and start one thing at a time, while the live getting-started guide still foregrounds compound deposits, bonds, loans, capital gains, dividends, passive income, eight professions, business, stocks, shop and casino, and frames the long-term endpoint as becoming a “representative capitalist.”

The growth plan now needs one canonical public story that connects acquisition → pre-signup value → signup reason → first meaningful action → D1/D7/D30 continuity without making wealth accumulation or gambling-style excitement the primary purpose of Moneyverse.

## Changes

- Added `PUBLIC_CONSUMER_NARRATIVE_GROWTH_SPEC.md` and Korean counterpart.
- Defined the canonical public promise: **start one meaningful thread → make it yours → see it grow → return to continue it**.
- Defined first-30-second, first-three-minute and first-session consumer outcomes.
- Reframed jobs, collections, virtual stocks, banking/loans, businesses, casino/minigames and seasons around identity, learning, mastery, curation and continuity rather than passive-income/wealth-maximization messaging.

- Added viral/share rules that prioritize collection, space, profession, season, learning and community artifacts rather than wealth/debt/casino/return cards.

- Added security/privacy review for public/private leakage, share-link phishing, finance-like claim drift and referral farming.
