## Why

The strongest current consumer-growth gap is a recurring public reason to revisit. Production home and `/announcements` are reachable, but both currently state that reviewed public news is still being prepared. The planned content return loop therefore is not yet observable as a live recurring product.

## Changed

- Defined the Weekly World Brief as a 1–3 minute consumer product rather than a generic announcement feed.
- Standardized a concise editorial structure: one-sentence week summary, roughly three meaningful changes, one learning point, one contextual action and one catch-up line.
- Defined separate continuation for first-time anonymous, new signed-in, active, lapsed and long-term users.
- Connected the brief to first-30-second understanding, first 3 minutes, D1/D3/D7/D14/D30 and long-term archive/identity.
- Added an SEO funnel from qualified impression through activation, D7/D30 and retention-adjusted contribution.
- Restricted indexability to substantial public content; thin recaps, private economy/account pages and ad-inventory pages stay out.
- Added content-led social sharing rules and recipient-first share landing behavior.
- Kept monetization after useful value and prohibited sponsor influence over WDX outcomes, loan terms, ranking or moderation.
- Added KPI/cohort framework and five experiments.
- Added security/privacy/abuse guardrails for public/private leakage, phishing impersonation, finance-like claim drift, referral farming, UGC abuse and analytics/ad overcollection.
- Preserved all existing auth/session/RBAC/ledger/admin/privacy boundaries.

## Research adopted

- Spotify Newsroom, 2026-07-10: predictable weekly discovery cadence + user control.
- Discord, 2026-08-20: discovery/social context should be judged by downstream play and retention, not impressions alone.
- Google Search Central, 2026-06-03 / worldwide rollout noted 2026-08-31: generative-AI visibility reporting is an additional discovery diagnostic.
- Naver Search Advisor current 2026 guidance: visitor-first content, clear titles/descriptions, explicit index separation, anti-thin/mass-generated/traffic-manipulation rules.
- Google Search Central 2026-08-28 Site Reputation Policy and current FTC native-ad/endorsement principles are retained as sponsor/editorial guardrails.
