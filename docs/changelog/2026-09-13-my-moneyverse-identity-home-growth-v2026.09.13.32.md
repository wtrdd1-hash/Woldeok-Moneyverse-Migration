## Why this change

The largest remaining growth gap was not another feature. Moneyverse lacked a compact consumer relationship model that answers: who am I becoming here, what have I meaningfully built, and what should I continue next?

This became higher priority after runtime recovery showed that the current home and guide are still strongly feature- and accumulation-oriented while recent growth plans emphasize identity, learning, collection, curation, community contribution and long-term history.

## What changed

Added English canonical `MY_MONEYVERSE_IDENTITY_HOME_GROWTH_SPEC.md` and Korean counterpart.

The new plan:
- defines **My Moneyverse** as 2–3 identity signals + one active thread + one history proof + one next chapter;
- updates visitor, D0–D3, D7–D30, long-term and comeback home priorities;
- defines identity-signal constraints, editability and privacy expectations;
- connects the model to activation, D1/D3/D7/D14/D30 and multi-season retention;
- adds viral, SEO, monetization and trust guardrails;
- adds five experiments and new relationship/identity KPIs;
- records the first recovered-service consumer `Runtime Product Reality Audit`.

## Security/privacy/legal impact

High-risk planning gates recorded for:
- private-history/profile leakage;
- overreaching behavioral identity inference;
- phishing/impersonation around personalized comeback/share pages.

Minimum conditions include private-by-default personalized history, public-safe fields only, editable/rejectable non-sensitive identity labels, first-party-domain trust, no secret-bearing URLs, and separate QA before public personalized surfaces.

WLD/WDX remain virtual/simulated/game-only. New behavioral advertising, material referral rewards, cross-service tracking, public personalized UGC or minor-targeted monetization require separate privacy/legal/trust review.

## Profitability/SEO/viral impact

Monetization is positioned after identity attachment through ad-free subscription and non-P2W expression/curation products. Personalized identity labels, finance outcomes, moderation priority, casino advantage and social legitimacy are not saleable.

My Moneyverse itself is not an SEO page factory. Only substantial public archives, guides, fictional-company histories, collection/lore material and opt-in showcase stories are index candidates.
