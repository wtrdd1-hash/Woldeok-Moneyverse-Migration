# Changelog — Retention-to-Viral Growth Loop v2026.09.13.23

Date: 2026-09-13
Type: documentation-only consumer growth planning

## Why

The current planning stack defines pre-signup activation and a D1→D30 return ladder, but retained-user value was not yet connected tightly enough to high-quality acquisition. Referral mechanics alone would create spam/fraud pressure and could over-reward raw registrations.

## Changed

- Added `RETENTION_TO_VIRAL_GROWTH_LOOP_SPEC.md` as the English canonical specification.
- Added Korean counterpart `RETENTION_TO_VIRAL_GROWTH_LOOP_SPEC.ko.md`.
- Defined the loop `meaningful progress → owner-first artifact → optional share → value-first public landing → signup/activation → D7`.
- Prioritized weekly/monthly recap, collection, profession, space, season, learning and community artifacts over wealth/profit/status-only cards.
- Defined lifecycle-specific artifact opportunities for D1–D3, D7, D14 and D30+.
- Defined a value-first non-member share landing that does not immediately hide the shared content behind authentication.
- Made referral secondary to useful artifact sharing and prohibited meaningful economic reward for raw signup.
- Added creator/community formats with sponsorship/material-connection disclosure requirements.
- Added SEO quality rules: substantial opt-in public archives may be index candidates; thin auto-generated recap/referral/private financial-game pages should normally remain noindex/private.
- Added monetization placement principles that preserve share/recipient value and prohibit financial-game advantage.
- Added trust review for private-data leakage, phishing/impersonation, referral farming, UGC harassment, creator deception and analytics/ad overcollection.
- Added owner-side retention, sharing-quality, viral-efficiency, brand/content, revenue-quality and trust-guardrail KPIs.
- Added five experiments: owner-first recap, story landing vs signup wall, self-progress vs wealth/rank sharing, retained-milestone referral gating, and selective high-quality SEO indexing.

## External research reviewed

Research date: 2026-09-13.

- Spotify 2026 Investor Day and 2025 Wrapped official product material: personalized recap as owner value, cultural sharing and acquisition/brand surface. Directly adopted as a pattern, not copied mechanically.
- Discord GDC 2026 and August 2026 social/discovery updates: social context and game discovery can reinforce play frequency and reach. Used as directional evidence only.
- TradingView 2026 community paper-trading contests: simulated practice can create creator/community participation. Real-cash prizes and profit-only social ranking were explicitly not adopted.
- Google Search Central current Search Appearance/ProfilePage and August 2026 Site Reputation Policy update: public creator/community surfaces should be substantial, accurate and not reputation-abuse inventory.
- Naver Search Advisor current sitemap/RSS and URL inspection guidance: intentional discoverable public content and index-quality observation preferred over thin URL growth.
- FTC review/testimonial guidance and 2025–2026 enforcement: incentives must not require positive sentiment and material connections/incentives require clear disclosure.

## Security/privacy impact

High risks recorded:
1. accidental publication/indexing of private balances, portfolio, account/security or social data;
2. phishing/impersonation of Moneyverse share/referral/reward destinations;
3. referral farming and multi-account reward abuse.

Minimum conditions are documented. Actual runtime security changes are not part of this update and require separate development/QA before launch of affected surfaces.

## Legal/consumer impact

- WLD/WDX remain virtual/simulated/game-only.
- No real investment return, cash-out or guaranteed-return framing was added.
- Creator sponsorship/incentive disclosures remain required where applicable.
- Personalized advertising, minors, new tracking/contact-upload behavior and expanded public UGC require separate privacy/legal/trust review.

## SEO impact

The plan favors substantial public archives, collection/lore/season/community retrospectives and original educational content. Thin generated recap URLs, private financial-game pages, referral/reward pages and sensitive account surfaces are not SEO inventory.

## Revenue impact

The plan creates monetizable expression around profile/space/archive/showcase cosmetics and ad removal after repeated value. Success is evaluated using D7/D30, recipient quality, subscription conversion, ad-induced churn, LTV and contribution margin rather than impressions alone.
