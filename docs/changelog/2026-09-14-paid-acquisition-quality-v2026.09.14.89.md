## Consumer planning
- Defined the largest current gap as paid acquisition quality: preventing optimization toward clicks, cheap signups or finance-curious traffic that fails to become a healthy D7/D30 user.
- Added the loop `truthful paid creative → source-matched public-safe landing → one proof/sample → authored interest → contextual signup → meaningful activation → D1 promise kept → D7 retained intent → D30 durable history → contribution margin → scale`.
- Added safe promise families for persistent-world identity, collection/lore/season discovery, learning/simulation and community/cooperative projects.
- Excluded cold-acquisition framing around real profit, high yield, guaranteed returns, loss recovery, debt urgency, casino wins and raw WLD giveaways.
- Added a creative→landing→product continuity contract and human review requirement for automated creative expansion.
- Added D1/D3/D7/D14/D30 quality expectations for paid cohorts.

## Economics and KPI changes
- Added `CAC_activation`, fraud-adjusted `CAC_D7`, `CAC_D30`, retained contribution, payback and mature-cohort LTV/CAC gates.
- Added paid-source activation, retention, promise comprehension, authored interest, first-sample completion and campaign/creative/landing cohort metrics.
- Added invalid traffic, fake signup, affiliate/referral fraud, finance-like misunderstanding, privacy complaint, phishing report, sensitive-targeting and ad-induced churn guardrails.
- Scale now requires healthy downstream retention and plausible contribution, not cheap registration volume.

## Experiments
- Source-matched landing vs generic homepage.
- Signup optimization vs meaningful-activation optimization.
- Broad discovery vs qualified intent controls.
- Platform attribution only vs incrementality-informed budget review.
- Immediate retargeting vs value-triggered retargeting.

## SEO / viral / monetization
- Reuse substantial canonical public content as paid destinations instead of creating thin doorway landing farms.
- Separate paid and referral economics; no meaningful WLD/WDX for raw click/signup/share/invite acceptance.
- Keep paid users behind the same retention-safe monetization gates as organic users; do not increase first-session ad load to recover CAC.

## Security, abuse and privacy
- HIGH: paid-ad impersonation/phishing/ATO.
- HIGH: automated creative drift into real-finance/gambling claims.
- HIGH: invalid traffic, bot/fake-signup and referral arbitrage.
- HIGH: private economy/social/security data leakage to advertising/analytics vendors.
- MEDIUM: landing mismatch, cloaking and affiliate deception.
- Existing OAuth/session/RBAC/admin/ledger/market-integrity/privacy/community boundaries remain unchanged; no security code changed.
