## Largest gap closed
Creator/community collaboration existed as a tactic, but there was no complete quality model from partner selection through first 30 seconds, pre-signup proof, activation and D7/D30 retention.

## Product decisions
- Prefer audience/product fit over follower count.
- Rank playable creator stories and authored community artifacts above giveaway-first acquisition.
- Preserve creator/source intent through landing, signup and D7.
- Judge partnerships on meaningful activation, retained CAC, D7/D30, LTV/contribution and trust—not raw reach/signups.
- Keep raw creator click/follow/share/signup free of meaningful WLD/WDX economic rewards by default.
- Require clear sponsor/material-connection disclosure and game-only framing near finance-adjacent creative.

## Experiments
1. contextual creator landing vs generic home;
2. authored artifact challenge vs follow/repost giveaway;
3. niche-fit creator vs broad-reach creator;
4. explicit sponsorship framing vs ambiguous framing;
5. reward-free attribution vs raw-signup economic bounty.

## Security/trust
High risks recorded: creator/official impersonation and phishing; referral/giveaway multi-account farming; finance-like creator deception; personalized showcase public/private leakage.
Medium risks: purchased/fake engagement, harassment/doxxing, analytics overcollection.
