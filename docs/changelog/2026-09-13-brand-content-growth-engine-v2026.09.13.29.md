## Security / privacy / abuse

High risks:
- accidental public/private data leakage;
- UGC/community spotlight impersonation, phishing or doxxing;
- sponsor/creator deception in financial-like fictional-market contexts.

Minimum protections: explicit public scope/consent, public-safe fields only, no private balances/portfolio/security/recovery data, report/takedown readiness, clear sponsor disclosure, and separate runtime QA before personalized public surfaces or scaled UGC spotlights.

No existing auth/session/RBAC/ledger/admin/privacy boundary is relaxed.
