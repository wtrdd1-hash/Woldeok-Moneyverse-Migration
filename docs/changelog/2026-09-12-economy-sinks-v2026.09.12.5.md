## Policy alignment

This revision follows the unlimited-by-default planning policy. Product participation, progression and normal purchasing should not be constrained by arbitrary hard caps. Protective constraints remain permitted only where required for security, abuse prevention, integrity, stability, legal requirements or genuinely finite inventory.
