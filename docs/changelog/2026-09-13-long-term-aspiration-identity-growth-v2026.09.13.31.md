## Why

The growth stack already covers acquisition, pre-signup value, D1–D30 return reasons, content-to-habit continuation, sharing and recurring brand content. The largest remaining gap was D30+ attachment: the product had many long-term systems but no unified consumer-facing answer to what a user is building over months.

## Security / privacy / abuse

High risks recorded:
1. public archive/profile leakage of private balances, portfolio interests, social graph, account/security/recovery data;
2. prestige/community manipulation via bots, collusion or multi-accounting;
3. phishing/impersonation through recap/share pages.

Minimum planning conditions:
- public-safe fields only and explicit opt-in publication;
- no meaningful economic reward for raw views/shares/rank;
- clear first-party branding/domain and no secret-bearing shared URLs;
- separate development/security QA before new personalized public surfaces, economic prestige rewards or new deep-link/share auth flows.

No security code was changed.

## SEO / viral / monetization impact

SEO:
- index substantial season archives, fictional-company histories, collection/lore guides and opt-in public showcases;
- do not mass-index thin user recap cards, private asset histories, portfolios or referral/reward pages.

Viral:
- prefer multi-season stories, collections, spaces, learning and community legacy over raw invite links or wealth displays.

Monetization:
- prioritize non-P2W presentation, archive/museum/space cosmetics and ad-free subscription after repeated value;
- reject economic advantage disguised as prestige;
- treat hard-to-cancel subscription behavior as a trust failure.
