# v2026.09.22.344 — Stock mutation step-up

## English canonical
High-impact administrator stock mutations now require recent reauthentication in addition to the existing admin session and CSRF boundary. Manual price changes, trading halt/settlement, halt-settlement retry, and corporate actions are covered. Read-only stock operations and lower-risk catalog editing are unchanged.
