## 1. Executive Summary & Philosophy

This specification defines the authoritative architecture for state governance, State-Owned Enterprises (SOEs), State Holding Corporations, and the autonomous private enterprise ecosystem in Woldeok Moneyverse.

### 1.1 Global Benchmark References
1. **Singapore Temasek Model**:
   - The state-owned holding corporation (`WSHC`, Woldeok State Holding Corporation) manages state commercial interests and strategic assets independently from political bureaucracy.
2. **Norway GPFG Governance & Transparency**:
   - Compliant with the Santiago Principles and Truman Scoreboard; public disclosure of all financial statements, dividend flows, and management evaluations.
3. **Management Evaluation System**:
   - Hourly evaluation across self-revenue ratio, financial soundness, and public utility contribution resulting in letter grades (S/A/B/C/D/E) linked to performance incentives and dividend rates.
4. **Autonomous Private Venture IPO Pipeline**:
   - Seamless progression from Seed Venture -> Series A/B -> Automated IPO on WDX Stock Exchange, paying 15% corporate tax and paying shareholder dividends.
