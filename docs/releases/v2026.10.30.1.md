Status: source candidate until Test, PR, Production and Telegram receipt are verified.

## Root cause
At 2026-10-30 09:00 KST, moneyverse-telegram-market.timer was active/enabled, yet SubState=elapsed and NextElapseUSecMonotonic=infinity. No virtual stock news/price/error scans had run since 2026-10-29 22:28:53 KST. The OnBootSec / OnUnitInactiveSec-only timer exhausted its schedule after an earlier manual service run. The base operations timer ran normally but only checked the market timer's active flag, so the outage was silent.

## Fixes
- Explicit systemd OnCalendar every two minutes, with Persistent catch-up; confirm future realtime trigger.
- Existing operations bot now verifies that the market timer is waiting with a finite monotonic OR realtime next trigger. An active but elapsed timer is CRITICAL; recovery is alerted on restoration.
- Store observed_at for successful market snapshots. Report an un-timestamped legacy checkpoint or monitoring gap over 10 minutes; never mislabel a many-hour price difference as a two-minute spike.
- Allow the virtual-market 09:00 KST daily digest to recover until 11:59 KST; no night-time or initial-baseline fake recap.
- Keep existing safe read-only PostgreSQL market/AI news sources, private bot pairing, duplicate protection and sanitized critical error log summaries unchanged.
