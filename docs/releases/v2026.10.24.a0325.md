## Contract

A PostgreSQL-resolved anchor preserves full `(created_at, id)` ordering even at microsecond precision; unknown/foreign cursors return no rows. The actor always comes from the authenticated session. The mobile proxy forwards the query and cookie but forcibly sets `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and drops ETag/Last-Modified. Monetary amounts remain canonical integer strings.
