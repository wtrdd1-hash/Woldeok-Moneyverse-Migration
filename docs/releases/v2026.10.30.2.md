Status: Production LIVE, PR #1966 merged at 209fb65bfab22d5c937ff7d11bb73bb599ca2c4e.

## Root cause
At 2026-10-30 09:10 KST, the host had ~3.03 GB available on the 99 GB system volume and ~5.77 GB on the 197 GB second volume. Linux df reported approximately 97%/98% utilized. The original bot computed used/total (~93%), under its 95% critical threshold, reporting only WARNING because the free/reserved blocks were not accounted for.

## Fix
- Use the fraction of storage not available to the unprivileged service: (total - available_free) / total, consistent with df effective fullness.
- CRITICAL at 95% or higher; WARNING at 90–94%; normal otherwise. Include the rounded effective percentage in messages.
- Keep the existing 2-minute base Telegram operating timer and independent market timer, client pairing, idempotent transition alerts and redaction.
