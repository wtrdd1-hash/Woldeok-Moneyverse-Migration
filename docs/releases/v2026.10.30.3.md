Status: candidate until CI/Test/Production/Telegram receipt evidence is recorded.

## Defect
At 2026-10-30 09:20 KST, a healthy market timer briefly entered systemd SubState=running while its scheduled oneshot executed. The base Telegram watchdog v2026.10.30.1 required waiting with a finite next trigger even during legitimate execution. It sent a false CRITICAL unscheduled:running notification, then recovered when the two-minute calendar timer returned waiting with the next scheduled 09:22 event.

## Fix

- Monitor the independent moneyverse-telegram-market.service Result as a separate critical signal: a failed oneshot remains CRITICAL despite an otherwise scheduled timer. The systemd worker has TimeoutStartSec 45 seconds.
- Preserve the existing send/recovery deduplication, 2-minute schedule, news/price logic and private-chat safety.
