# v2026.09.16.4 — Searchable stock trade history

## User-facing change
Members can open `/stocks/history` to search their virtual-stock trade history by symbol and filter it to buys or sells. Results expose quantity, execution price, gross amount, tax, timestamp, and a direct link back to the stock hub. The existing market history card now links to this focused workspace.

## Integrity and privacy
This is a read-only view over the existing actor-scoped `/api/v1/stocks/history` endpoint. It does not change the ledger, trade execution, authorization, idempotency, database schema, migrations, or Production data. WLD amounts remain integer-string data.

## Validation gate
Merge/promotion requires CI plus exact-SHA isolated Test verification of authenticated history ownership, search/filter combinations, empty/no-result states, responsive/accessibility behavior, private noindex policy, and candidate image/SHA parity.
