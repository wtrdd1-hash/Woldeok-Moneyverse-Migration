# Virtual Businesses

Businesses are long-horizon virtual-economy assets that sit between short repeatable work and market speculation.

## Economy role

A business can have:

- acquisition/ownership state;
- operating values and revenue/cost measures;
- distributions/dividends where implemented;
- member ownership/read models;
- progression/quest interactions.

## Balance principle

Business payback should be measured against other faucets/sinks such as work rewards, deposit interest, loans and shop prices. If a business can be financed by a cheaper credit path and repay itself almost immediately, it becomes an arbitrage faucet rather than a progression asset.

The September balance review compared business payback horizons with credit and banking policy and found the primary drift in the loan path rather than inventing arbitrary new business prices.

## Exact money

Revenue, costs, profit, acquisition prices and distributions use exact WLD handling. Admin displays must not convert large values to unsafe JavaScript numbers.

## Data safety

Historical ownership/distribution records should be preserved. Balance changes should modify current policy/contracts prospectively unless an explicit migration states otherwise.
